I'm a dedicated Garry's Mod developer who focuses on building polished, playable systems with GLua. I put a lot of attention into how addons behave in actual gameplay, from NPC AI and combat to reusable framework code.

My work includes a PvZ NPC addon and the integration of DrGBase with 308 Base. I enjoy taking complicated systems, cleaning them up, and turning them into cohesive tools and experiences for Garry's Mod.
