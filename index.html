About

I'm a Garry's Mod developer passionate about creating polished, functional addons and frameworks. I work extensively with GLua to build custom entities, NPCs, SWEPs, gamemodes, and gameplay systems that enhance the GMod experience.

Specializations:

  • Custom NPCs & AI

  • Addon creation
